Data sources / 数据来源

Open-Meteo weather data (https://open-meteo.com/) is fetched online at runtime under CC BY 4.0 and is not distributed with the installer. 中国行政区划名称与中心点坐标来自阿里云 DataV.GeoAtlas (https://datav.aliyun.com/portal/school/atlas/area_selector)，以内嵌的 china-regions.json 随 GlassDesk.Platform.dll 分发，仅用于城市选择与就近匹配。Approximate IP location is queried at runtime from myip.ipip.net and ipwho.is; neither service is distributed with the installer. Open-Meteo、myip.ipip.net 与 ipwho.is 均仅在运行时联网查询。
